Privacy Policy

Last updated: Mar 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to WaterTracker when distributed on Google Play. It explains what data is collected, how it is used, and the choices available to users. By using WaterTracker, you agree to the practices described in this policy, subject to applicable local laws and Google Play requirements.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data - Email address and basic profile information when you sign in with email/password or Google (via Firebase Auth).
  • Hydration logs - Amount of water you record, container type (for example, glass or bottle), and timestamps for each entry.
  • App configuration - Your daily goal, notification preferences, and other in-app settings.

2. How we use your data

  • To display your daily, weekly, and monthly hydration progress.
  • To send optional reminders, if you enable notifications.
  • To keep your data synced across devices using Firebase.

3. Data storage and third parties

  • WaterTracker uses Google Firebase (Firebase Auth and Cloud Firestore) as the backend.
  • Your data is stored on Firebase servers subject to Google's terms and privacy policy.
  • We do not sell your personal data, and this sample app does not include third-party ad SDKs.

4. Your choices and controls

  • You can adjust or disable notifications from within the app or your device settings.
  • You may request deletion of your account data by contacting the developer or removing your Firebase account data directly if you control the project.

5. Developer responsibilities

If you fork or publish this project, you are responsible for updating this policy to accurately describe your own infrastructure, analytics, logging, and any additional data processing you introduce.

6. Contact

For questions about this sample app or its privacy approach, update this section with your preferred contact email or website.

This text is a starting point only and does not constitute legal advice.
